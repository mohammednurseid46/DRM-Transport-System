# Dream More Transport System – Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as developers, contributors, and maintainers of the **Dream More Transport System** pledge to make participation in our project and community a constructive, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and collaborate in ways that foster an open, professional, welcoming, and productive software engineering environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive, reliable project environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and mutual respect toward collaborators
* Giving and receiving constructive architectural, code, and UI/UX feedback professionally
* Prioritizing data integrity, safety, and privacy in transit and passenger-handling logic
* Taking ownership, apologizing promptly for breaking changes or unintentional oversights, and actively working to resolve issues
* Focusing on what is best for the overall reliability and usability of the transport system

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, abusive comments, insulting language, and personal or political attacks
* Disruptive repository vandalism, deliberate injection of malicious code, or uncoordinated destructive migrations
* Public or private harassment of fellow developers or platform users
* Publishing others' private information, API keys, credentials, or production connection strings without explicit authorization
* Any conduct reasonably deemed unprofessional, discriminatory, or harmful in a collaborative engineering setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers will take prompt, fair, and appropriate action in response to any behavior deemed harmful, offensive, or destructive to the repository or community.

Maintainers retain the right and authority to review, edit, reject, or revert comments, issues, pull requests, and commit contributions that fail to align with this Code of Conduct, communicating moderation reasons when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all repository spaces (GitHub/GitLab issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews) and during any official representation of the Dream More Transport System project in external or public channels.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project leads will follow these guidelines when determining consequences for violations:

### 1. Correction

* **Impact**: Use of unprofessional language or minor disruptive conduct in pull requests, issues, or commit logs.
* **Consequence**: A private, written notification detailing the issue and clarifying expected standards.

### 2. Warning

* **Impact**: Repeated unprofessional behavior or a single non-trivial breach of collaboration etiquette.
* **Consequence**: A formal warning with specific conditions for continued participation. Continued violations will escalate to contributor restrictions.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

* **Impact**: A serious violation of standards, sustained hostility, or intentional disregard for code review processes.
* **Consequence**: A temporary ban from opening issues, commenting, or pushing pull requests for a specified duration.

### 4. Permanent Ban

* **Impact**: Persistent harassment, security compromise, intentional data sabotage, or malicious conduct.
* **Consequence**: Permanent revocation of repository access and complete ban from the project community.
